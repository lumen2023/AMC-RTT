## Three Claim Levels / 三类 Claim

### Supported / 已支持

These are scoped empirical claims tied to named artifacts and protocols.

这些是绑定到具体 artifact 与 protocol 的有范围经验结论。

### Not Supported / 不支持

These are claims whose required gates failed or have not been run.

这些是所需门控失败或尚未执行的结论。

### Future Required Evidence / 未来所需证据

Every stronger claim must name its next falsifiable gate rather than rely on
prose escalation.

每个更强 claim 都必须指出下一步可证伪门控，不能靠文字升级。
